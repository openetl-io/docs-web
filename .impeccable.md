## Design Context

### Users
Developers learning, running, integrating, and extending OpenETL. They need a clear starting point, searchable reference material, and documentation that matches their version.

### Brand Personality
Clear, practical, precise. Keep the voice grounded in the open-source repositories and avoid promotional claims.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a calm, precise direction as the working assumption for this open-ended improvement request. Preserve the existing logo and locally hosted Geist fonts. Use graphite neutrals, a restrained green accent, generous spacing, and strong typographic hierarchy, with complete light and dark themes.

### Design Principles
- Put finding the right documentation first.
- Keep section names, descriptions, versions, and destinations driven by fetched content.
- Use typography and spacing to establish hierarchy, with minimal decoration.
- Make navigation usable with keyboards, touch, and reduced motion.
- Keep the site static and fast; add no dependencies for presentation alone.
